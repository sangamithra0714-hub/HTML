VIJAY

Vijay is an Indian actor and playback singer who works in Tamil cinema. He is one of the highest-paid actors in Tamil cinema and has a huge fan following. Vijay has won several awards for his performances, including Tamil Nadu State Film Awards and Filmfare Awards South. He is known for his charismatic screen presence, dance skills, and philanthropic activities.

Vijay made his acting debut as a child artist in the film "Vetri" (1984) and later appeared in several films as a lead actor. Some of his notable films include "Ghilli," "Thuppakki," "Mersal," and "Master." He has also lent his voice to several songs in his films, showcasing his talent as a playback singer.
